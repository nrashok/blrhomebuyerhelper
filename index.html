How it works

Load a skill into Claude Code or claude.ai, describe your property, and start sharing documents.

1

Start a session

Tell Claude the property details — project name, survey number, K-RERA ID — and what documents you have.

2

Share documents

Upload documents as you collect them: K-RERA certificate, sale agreement, cost sheet, EC, khata, RTC, and more.

3

Track findings

Claude cross-checks each document against the checklist and maintains a live tracker: Verified, Flagged, or Open.

4

Get a report

Request a structured report: headline verdict, section-by-section walkthrough, and a close-out list of what's still needed.

You

Claude
1.1 ✓ Verified for the deed chain • 1.2 ✓ Verified for the 30-year EC • 4.1 ⚠ Flagged — a brochure's "approved layout" label is not enough without the exact layout sanction and survey-number match. Highest priority Open: 2.2 DC conversion, 2.3 kharab/gramathana classification, 4.4 lake/buffer checks.
You
I uploaded the RTC and the layout sanction. The RTC still shows agricultural status, and there is no DC conversion order.
Claude
2.2 ⚠ Flagged • 2.3 ⚠ Flagged — direct conflict between the claimed residential use and the RTC's agricultural classification. 4.1 ✓ Verified only once the survey number and sanctioned layout match exactly. 4.4 ⚠ Flagged — parcel is in a sensitive zone.

The plot is still agricultural in the RTC, has no conversion order, and the layout sanction cannot cure a land-classification defect. Scenario 4: Revenue site near Devanahalli — PTCL / government land risk

You
I found a 30x40 revenue site near Devanahalli, close to the airport. The broker says it's a "converted site with clear title." He showed me a GPA (General Power of Attorney) and a sale deed. Price is well below market. Should I go ahead?
Claude
Multiple red flags here. I'll need the survey number to check Bhoomi for RTC and mutation records. A GPA-based chain is high-risk — Karnataka courts have ruled that GPA alone does not transfer title. Let me see the documents.
You
Survey No. 45/3, Devanahalli hobli. Here's the GPA and the sale deed. The RTC shows "Sarkar Jamin" (government land) under the land type column.
Claude
1.1 ⚠ Flagged — GPA-based title chain is not a valid conveyance in Karnataka • 2.1 ⚠ Flagged — "Sarkar Jamin" in the RTC means this is classified as government land • 2.3 ⚠ Flagged — no valid DC conversion can exist on government land • 1.4 ⚠ Flagged — the below-market price is consistent with a disputed or encumbered parcel.
Verdict: WALK-AWAY

This is government land (Sarkar Jamin/PTCL). No private sale is legally possible regardless of what documents the broker produces. A GPA does not transfer ownership. Do not pay any advance. If money has already been paid, consult a lawyer immediately.

Under-construction apartment in Electronic City — delayed possession and K-RERA expiry

You
Looking at a 3BHK in Chamundi Towers, Phase 2, Electronic City. Developer: Tungabhadra Infra Pvt Ltd. They promised possession by Dec 2024 but it's still not ready. K-RERA no. PRM/KA/RERA/1234/2020. I have the sale agreement, K-RERA certificate, payment schedule, and two demand letters from 2025.
Claude
Delayed possession is a major risk area. I'll check: (1) whether the K-RERA registration is still active or expired, (2) what the sale agreement says about delay penalties, and (3) whether the builder has applied for a K-RERA extension. Let me review the documents.
You
Documents uploaded. The K-RERA certificate shows validity until March 2025. The sale agreement has a delay penalty clause of Rs. 5/sqft/month. The demand letters from the builder ask for the next tranche despite no possession.
Claude
3.1 ⚠ Flagged — K-RERA registration expired March 2025; the project cannot legally sell or collect payments without renewal • 15.1 ⚠ Flagged — possession date missed by 18+ months, no extension filed on K-RERA portal • 15.3 ⚠ Flagged — demand letters after expiry are a violation of RERA Act Section 3 • 14.3 ✓ Verified — delay penalty clause exists but at Rs. 5/sqft/month it is well below the SBI MCLR-linked rate RERA prescribes. Highest priority Open: 2.5 OC status, 3.3 Fire NOC, 4.2 structural completion percentage.
Verdict: HIGH RISK — do not pay further installments

The K-RERA registration has expired. Under the RERA Act, the builder cannot collect further payments or advertise until renewed. File a complaint on the K-RERA portal. Get a lawyer to calculate the correct delay compensation (should be SBI MCLR-linked, not the sub-market Rs. 5/sqft clause). Do not pay the demand letters.

3

Upload documents & start verifying

Share your property documents one by one or all at once. Claude checks each against the full checklist and tracks findings as Verified, Flagged, or Open.

📄

Title & ownership documents

Upload these first — they form the foundation of any property check.

  • Sale deed / mother deed — proves ownership chain
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) — 30-year from Kaveri 2.0
  • RTC (Record of Rights) — from Bhoomi portal
  • Khata certificate / extract — A-khata or e-Khata from BBMP/e-Aasthi
  • Property tax receipts — confirms khata is active
🏗

Approvals & conversion documents

Critical for plots and villas — confirms the land is legally buildable.

  • DC conversion order — agricultural to non-agricultural
  • Layout sanction — BDA / BMRDA / BIAPPA approval
  • Building plan approval — from BBMP or local authority
  • Occupancy Certificate (OC) — for completed buildings
  • K-RERA certificate — mandatory for projects > 8 units
💰

Financial & agreement documents

Protects you from hidden charges and one-sided clauses.

  • Sale agreement / allotment letter — terms, area, penalties
  • Cost sheet — base price + all add-on charges
  • Payment schedule — milestone-linked or time-linked
  • Brochure / price list — treated as marketing, not proof
  • Loan sanction letter — if the bank has done its own check
🔎

What Claude checks for you

The skill cross-checks every document against these categories.

  • Title chain continuity — gaps, GPA fraud, PTCL land
  • Land classification — agricultural vs non-agricultural in RTC
  • Approval legitimacy — layout sanction matches survey no.
  • Document conflicts — brochure vs sale deed area mismatch
  • Sensitive zones — lake buffer, kharab, gramathana, BIAPPA
  • K-RERA compliance — registration, delays, expiry
  • Site-visit items — honestly kept as Open until you visit
